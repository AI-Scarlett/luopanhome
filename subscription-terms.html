Billing

Subscriptions are charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Renewal and Cancellation

Your Apple ID account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID subscription settings in the App Store.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple according to App Store policies.
